### Hi there 👋

I am an Assistant Professor at NASK National Research Institute in Warsaw, Poland, where I lead the [NLP Department](https://nask-nlp.github.io). My research focuses on building safe and trustworthy language technologies, with a particular emphasis on large language models, evaluation, and domain-specific NLP systems.

#### 🔬 Current Research
- LLM safety, evaluation, and red-teaming
- Domain-specific NLP (clinical, biomedical, and scholarly domains)
- Information retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Multilingual and low-resource NLP
- Literature review automation

I’m happy to hear from students interested in NLP and machine learning research on the topics above.

#### 🚀 Current Projects
- Developing PLLuM, an open Polish large language model focused on safety, reliability, and practical deployment
- Research on automated systematic literature reviews and scientific discovery systems
- Building domain-adapted NLP systems for specialised applications
